# Pack videos on the Hedge site

Each video is a short, silent loop that shows what one pack does. Each
poster is the still image that the page shows before the video plays. The
site build copies a video and its poster only when a page shows them.

| Pack | Video | Poster | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quality gates | `pull-requests.mp4` | `pull-requests.jpg` | 28.5 s |
| Orchestration | `orchestration.mp4` | `orchestration.jpg` | 30 s |
| Memory | `memory.mp4` | `memory.jpg` | 29 s |
| Hub | `hub.mp4` | `hub.jpg` | 28.5 s |
| Slack | `slack.mp4` | `slack.jpg` | 20 s |

All videos are 1280 x 720, H.264, with no audio.

The hedgehogs in the videos are PostHog's own art from PostHog's brand site,
[brand.posthog.com](https://brand.posthog.com). PostHog made them and owns
them. The videos show them unchanged, with credit, as `page/art/README.md`
says for the art on the site. The PostHog name and logo are trademarks of
PostHog Inc. The screens in the videos come from a demo project, not from
real users.

Do not draw, change, or generate a hedgehog or the PostHog logo. Do not edit
a video or a poster here. To change a video, replace the file with a new
approved cut and update its length in `page/cast.ts`.
