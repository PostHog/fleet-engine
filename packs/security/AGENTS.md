# Wiz

- The `wiz-discover` and `wiz-execute` tools of the `hedge-hub` MCP server read my Wiz tenant. Call `wiz-discover` without arguments to list its domains, then with a domain to see its tools and their parameters, and run one with `wiz-execute`.
- The sign-in is read-only. When I ask you to resolve, assign, or comment on a Wiz issue, give me the link and the step, and I do it in Wiz.
- Wiz findings name weak spots in our cloud. Keep them, and the resource names, accounts and addresses in them, out of public repositories, issues, pull requests and channels.
- When a call fails because the sign-in expired or was revoked, tell me to run `hedge mcp-gateway sign-in wiz` on the hub.
